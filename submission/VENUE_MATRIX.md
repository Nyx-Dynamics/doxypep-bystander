## Recommended sequence
1. **Now:** post to medRxiv with the Zenodo-archived repo linked (DOI in the data/code
   availability statement).
2. **Primary submission:** PLOS Biology Meta-Research, after the P0/P1 revisions — lead
   with the instrument, demote the trial autopsy (editor's item 1).
3. **If declined:** JAC-AMR (lightest reframe, keeps the ID spine).
4. EID only if you want to shed the meta-science/justice apparatus for a pure
   surveillance-gap-and-fix piece.

## Cross-cutting reframe that lowers risk at every venue
The editor and meta-science reviewer converge on one move: **lead with the measurement
instrument, not the DoxyPEP trial autopsy.** Restructure so the thesis is "measurement
inheritance — an evidence apparatus inherits the denominator, cadence, coding, and axis of
the question it was built for," with the DoxyPEP trial as the *worked case study*. This
single change (a) foregrounds the most bulletproof, most transferable result, (b) demotes
the per-carrier 40% and 5/16/28 flashpoints to a labelled sensitivity vignette, and (c)
makes the paper a method rather than a single grievance — satisfying PLOS's generality
requirement in the same stroke.
